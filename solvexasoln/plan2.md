Yeh bilkul zaroori aur realistic point hai. Free-tier setups mein cold starts, database limits, ya instance sleep hone ke issues aate hain, aur weekly live system check se confidence bhi fresh rehta hai ki live demos kabhi fail na hon.

Aapke saare requirements ko integrate karke, neeche **Detailed 14-Day Blueprint (Task-by-Task breakdown)** ready kiya hai. Har task ko **"Creator / Engineering Asset"** ke frame mein rakha gaya hai taaki interview ka pressure bilkul mehsus na ho.
