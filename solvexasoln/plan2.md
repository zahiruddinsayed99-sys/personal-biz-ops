### **Sheet Structure & Highlights**

1. **Daily Action Tracker Tab:**
* **14 Din ke 52 Micro-Blocks:** Har din ko 20–35 minute ke 4 discrete blocks mein baanta gaya hai.
* **Bite-sized Tasks:** System HLD design notes, private voice memos (no camera), clean repo docs, zero-pressure informal hellos, aur live deployment testing.
* **Status Dropdowns:** `Not Started`, `In Progress`, `Done`, `Rolled Over`.
* **Friction Level (1 to 5):** Introversion aur hesitation track karne ke liye (1 = Easy/Comfortable, 5 = Hesitation feel hui).
* **Deliverable Link & Win Note:** Har task ke baad link paste karne aur 1-line self-win note likhne ki space.


2. **Dashboard & Resources Tab:**
* **Dynamic Progress Bar & Metrics:** Jaise-jaise aap tasks ko `Done` karenge, scorecard aur progress bar automatically update honge.
* **Weekly Ops Checklist (Sunday Days 6 & 13):** E-Commerce critical user journey smoke test, Business Hub AI document processing verify karna, aur Cloud free-tier (Render/Railway/Supabase) memory logs/instance restart checklist.
* **Asset Repository Links:** Audio notes folder, video demos, aur technical chapters ko organize karne ke quick links.



---

### **Zero-Guilt Rule (To Protect Motivation):**

* Agar kisi din energy low ho ya koi block complete na ho, toh **Status ko `Rolled Over` kar dijiye bina kisi guilt ke**.
* Humara target perfection nahi, balki **roz 1 chhota win record karke momentum build karna** hai.

Aap sheet open karke check kar sakte hain! Jab aap ready hon, hum Day 1 ke pehle 30-minute block (E-Commerce HLD bullets draft) se start kar sakte hain.
