## NIFTY 50

Underlying: 1-minute chart · India market time (IST)

Proposed test rules

Trade the confirmed breakout direction, using the retest as the entry trigger.

1\. Opening range: 9:15–9:20 AM

Mark the highest high and lowest low of the first five 1-minute candles, including 9:15 through 9:19.

2\. Breakout / breakdown

- Bullish: a 1-minute candle closes above the range high.
- Bearish: a 1-minute candle closes below the range low.

3\. Wait for a retest

- Bullish: price returns to the range high and holds it as support.
- Bearish: price returns to the range low and rejects it as resistance.
- Enter only after a confirming candle closes in the breakout direction.

4\. Entry deadline: 10:30 AM

If no valid breakout and retest occur by 10:30 AM, record NO TRADE. Do not chase the move.
