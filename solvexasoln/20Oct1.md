# SwingPilot — SENSEX Intraday Strategy V1

Strategy type: Liquidity sweep + failed breakout reversal Status: Initial mechanical rules for historical backtesting — profitability not yet established.

We'll focus exclusively on situations where SENSEX moves beyond an identifiable high or low, fails to sustain the breakout, and shows evidence of reversing. We will use the 5-minute chart to identify context and the 1-minute chart to time entries.

## 2. Exact mechanical rules

### Step 1 — Mark liquidity levels on the 5-minute chart

Before looking for a trade, mark these levels:

1. Previous trading day's high and low.
2. The high and low of the 9:15–9:30 AM opening range.
3. Confirmed 5-minute swing highs and lows formed before the current setup.

For consistency, define a swing high as a candle whose high exceeds the highs of the two candles immediately before it and the two candles immediately after it. Define a swing low conversely. A swing is only confirmed after those two following candles close.

Use only levels that were identifiable at the time of the potential trade. Do not select levels retrospectively because they produced a good reversal.

Bearish sweep — potential PE

- Price trades above a marked liquidity high.
- A 5-minute candle closes back below that level.
- The highest price reached during the sweep becomes the sweep extreme.

A wick beyond the level followed by a close back inside qualifies as a potential sweep. It is not yet an entry signal.

### Step 3 — Confirm the reversal on the 1-minute chart

After the 5-minute sweep candle closes, switch to the 1-minute chart.

Bullish confirmation

1. Identify the most recent confirmed 1-minute swing high formed before the reversal.
2. Wait for a 1-minute candle to close above that swing high.
3. Record the broken swing level as the structure-break level.

Bearish confirmation

1. Identify the most recent confirmed 1-minute swing low formed before the reversal.
2. Wait for a 1-minute candle to close below that swing low.
3. Record the broken swing level.

This is the market-structure confirmation. A sweep without this confirmation is a no-trade.

### Step 4 — Entry on a retest

Wait for price to revisit the broken structure level.

- Bullish: price tests the broken swing high and a 1-minute candle closes above it.
- Bearish: price tests the broken swing low and a 1-minute candle closes below it.

Enter at the next available price after the confirmation candle closes. If the retest never occurs, do not chase the price.

For V1, all entries must occur between 9:30 and 11:00 AM IST.

## 3. Stop-loss, target and trade management

| Rule             | Bullish reversal             | Bearish reversal            |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Entry            | Confirmed bullish retest     | Confirmed bearish retest    |
| Stop-loss        | Below the sweep extreme      | Above the sweep extreme     |
| Risk \\(R\\)     | Entry − stop                 | Stop − entry                |
| Minimum target   | 1.5R above entry             | 1.5R below entry            |
| Preferred target | Next opposing liquidity high | Next opposing liquidity low |

For historical testing, use a small fixed buffer of 0.05 × the 5-minute ATR(14) beyond the sweep extreme. Calculate ATR using completed candles only. This is a starting parameter to test, not an optimized value.

The target must satisfy both conditions:

- At least 1.5 times the initial risk.
- The next opposing liquidity level must offer sufficient room to reach that target.

If the next opposing liquidity level is too close to allow 1.5R, record NO TRADE.

Time exit: close the simulated position after 60 minutes, or at 3:15 PM IST, whichever comes first. If neither stop nor target is hit, record the actual exit price at the time exit. This time exit is a backtest rule, not a recommendation to hold an option regardless of its premium behavior.

For this first test, allow a maximum of one trade per day. If both bullish and bearish setups occur, take only the first valid entry; record later setups but do not trade them in the primary test.

## 4. Mechanical no-trade rules

Record NO TRADE whenever any of the following applies:

- No marked liquidity level is swept.
- The 5-minute candle does not close back inside the level.
- The 1-minute structure break is missing.
- The retest or confirming close never occurs.
- The entry would occur outside the permitted window.
- The stop distance is zero, invalid or impractically large.
- The next liquidity target offers less than 1.5R.
- A prior trade has already been taken that day.

Do not loosen these conditions to make a historical setup qualify.

## 6. How we will judge V1

Do not judge the strategy by win rate alone. After collecting a meaningful sample, calculate:

- Number of eligible sessions and valid setups.
- Win rate and average win/loss in units of initial risk.
- Expectancy per trade after costs.
- Maximum drawdown and consecutive losses.
- Results separately for bullish and bearish sweeps.
- Whether the option premium results agree with the underlying-index signal.

We should keep these parameters unchanged during the initial sample, then test any revisions on a separate period to reduce overfitting.
