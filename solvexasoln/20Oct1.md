# Manual backtest using Zerodha Kite: NIFTY first, then SENSEX

We'll do this as a historical chart-by-chart verification, without coding. The objective is to check whether the 9:15 AM opening breakout gave a valid directional signal, whether it continued through 9:30 AM, and whether the corresponding ATM option could actually have produced a profit after costs.

One important distinction: a profitable index breakout is not automatically a profitable option trade. We'll record the index signal and option-premium result separately.

## 2. Important Kite limitation: index charts versus expired options

Zerodha's official documentation confirms two things:

- Kite charts support historical intraday candles, including one-minute candles.&#x20;

  [image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://support.zerodha.com\&sz=32)

- Kite does not provide historical charts for expired options contracts. That means you may be able to verify the NIFTY or SENSEX breakout on Kite, but you cannot reliably reconstruct every expired ATM option premium for the same date using Kite alone.&#x20;

This affects how we conduct the test. We'll first verify the index signal in Kite, then source historical option prices separately if necessary. We must not substitute the index movement for actual option P&L.

### B. Mark the opening range

For each session from 6 to 19 October:

1. Find the candle beginning at 9:15 AM IST.
2. Record its high and low.
3. Move to the next completed candle.
4. Mark the first candle that closes above the opening high or below the opening low.
5. Record the breakout time and price.
6. Inspect subsequent candles through 9:30 AM.
7. Record whether the move continued, reversed or remained inside the opening range.

## 5. How to inspect the ATM option premium

For any confirmed breakout:

1. Note the index level at the signal time.
2. Determine the nearest available ATM strike and select the corresponding Call or Put.
3. Identify the option contract's expiry for that historical date.
4. Record its entry premium and the premium at your defined exit time or stop.
5. Calculate the P&L using the applicable lot size and subtract estimated costs.

Kite's current option chain can display strikes and option charts for available contracts, but its historical-data limitation means expired options cannot be reconstructed directly in Kite.&#x20;
