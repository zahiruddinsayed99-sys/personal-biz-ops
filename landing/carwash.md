### Kaunsa Product Kahan Use Karna Hai?

* **Sun Shine Stain Remover with Polish**
* **Kahan use karein:** Car ki body, bike ki tanki, painted panels jahan ziddi daag hon (jaise paani ke dhabbe/hard water spots, bird droppings, tar ya exhaust ka dhuaan).
* **Kaam:** Ye ek mild compound-cum-polish hai jo purane dhabbe nikal kar paint me shine laata hai.
* **Dhyan rakhein:** Matte finish paint ya rough black plastic par bilkul mat lagana, warna safed dhabbe chhod dega.


* **Sun Shine Auto Guard Polish & Glass Cleaner**
* **Kahan use karein:** Car ki windshield, khidkiyan, side mirrors, headlight lenses, bike ka visor aur chrome parts (silencer/mirrors).
* **Kaam:** Glass ko bina streaks ke crystal clear karta hai aur paint/chrome par halki water-repellent (paani fisalne wali) shine deta hai.
* **Dhyan rakhein:** Glass saaf karte waqt spray direct kaanch par marne ke bajaye kapde par spray karke wipe karein.


* **Sun Shine Dashboard Shiner**
* **Kahan use karein:** Car ka dashboard, door pads (plastic parts), AC vents ke aaspas, rubber beading, bike ke unpainted black plastic mudguards aur side panels.
* **Kaam:** Dhoop se fade hue black plastic ko naya jaisa dark aur shiny look deta hai aur dhool jamne se rokta hai.
* **Strict Warning:** Steering wheel, accelerator/brake pedals, bike ke handle grips, seat aur tyres ke upar **galti se bhi mat lagana**—ye bohot slippery (fisaldar) hota hai aur accident ka risk ban sakta hai.

### Kaam Shuru Karne Ke Liye Kya Chahiye?

* 3 se 4 clean Microfiber cloths (sabse best rehte hain scratches se bachne ke liye).
* 1 foam sponge ya normal soft pad.

---

### Step-by-Step Kaam Kaise Karein

#### Step 0: Basic Tayyari (Sabse Zaroori)

* Car ya bike ko hamesha **chhaon (shade)** me khada karein. Dhoop me ya garam engine/bonnet par polish kabhi mat lagana.
* Pehle gadi ko normal paani aur shampoo se dho kar sukha lein taaki upar ki reti/dhool nikal jaye. Sukhi mitti par direct polish ragadne se scratches pad jayenge.

---

#### Step 1: Paint Ke Ziddi Daag Hatayein

**Product:** *Sun Shine Stain Remover with Polish*

1. Bottle ko acche se shake karein.
2. Microfiber cloth ya foam pad par coin (sikke) jitna liquid lein.
3. Jahan dhabbe ya dullness hai, wahan halka pressure dekar gol-gol (circular motion) 30-40 seconds ke liye rub karein.
4. Turant ek doosre sukhe microfiber kapde se use acche se wipe (buff) karke saaf kar dein. Sukhe chhodne par polish hard ho jati hai.

---

#### Step 2: Kaanch Aur Final Body Shine

**Product:** *Sun Shine Auto Guard Polish & Glass Cleaner*

1. **Glass aur Mirrors ke liye:**
* Apne clean microfiber kapde par 2-3 baar spray karein.
* Glass ko horizontally (seedha) wipe karein, fir kapde ko ulti side palat kar ek baar dry wipe kar lein—ekdum chamak jayega bina kisi nishan ke.


2. **Body Panels aur Chrome ke liye:**
* Thoda door se halka mist spray karein.
* Kapde se poore panel par failayein aur 10-15 seconds baad fast haath chala kar buff karein, ekdum mirror shine aa jayegi.



---

#### Step 3: Interior Aur Black Plastics Chamkayein

**Product:** *Sun Shine Dashboard Shiner*

1. **Car Interior:**
* Dashboard par direct spray na karein (warna glass par droplets chali jati hain). Sponge ya kapde par spray karein.
* Dashboard aur door plastics par evenly lagayein. Agar zyada chipchipa lage, toh 2 minute baad sukhe kapde se halka wipe kar lein taaki natural matte-satin finish mile.


2. **Bike Plastics:**
* Bike ke black mudguard aur side panels par sponge se lagayein.
* **Reminder:** Bike ki seat aur brake levers par bilkul na lage.



---

### 2 Pro Tips

* **Brakes bacha kar:** Bike ke alloy wheels ya disc plate ke paas spray karte waqt disc plate ko kapde ya cardboard se cover kar lein. Polish disc pad par lag gayi toh brake lagna band ho sakti hai.
* **Alag-alag kapde use karein:** Glass saaf karne wala kapda alag rakhein; agar dashboard shiner wala oily kapda windshield par lag gaya toh raat me headlight glare bohot pareshan karega.
