I’d suggest using a fictional company so your test data doesn't accidentally represent real company information. The structure below is based on common Indian digital-marketing agency services such as SEO, paid ads, social media, content marketing, and analytics. ([Fullscoop][1])

### Sample Document 3 — Good for testing search/RAG

**Title:** SEO Services for Indian Businesses

**Content:**

BharatGrowth Digital provides SEO services for businesses operating in the Indian market. The SEO program starts with a technical audit to identify indexing, crawling, website speed, mobile usability, metadata, internal linking, and structured-content issues.

Keyword research is performed according to search intent. Keywords are classified into informational, commercial, transactional, and local-intent categories.

The content team then creates topic clusters around important business areas. Each cluster contains a primary pillar page supported by related articles targeting specific customer questions.

For local businesses, the strategy includes Google Business Profile optimization, location-specific landing pages, local citations, review management, and geographically relevant content.

SEO performance is monitored using organic traffic, keyword visibility, impressions, clicks, leads, conversions, and revenue.

The expected timeline depends on website authority, competition, technical condition, content quality, and target keywords. SEO should generally be treated as a long-term acquisition channel rather than a short-term advertising campaign.
