📘 Retail Swing Trading Golden Rules & Tax Guide

Essential rules for Indian retail investors to remain disciplined and profitable.

🎯 1. The 1:2 Minimum Risk-to-Reward Rule

Never take a trade where potential reward is less than twice your risk. If your Stop Loss is 2.5%, your Target 1 must be at least 5.0%. This ensures profitability even with a 45% win rate.

🛑 2. Hard Stop Loss Non-Negotiable

Cut losses quickly. Never average down on a losing swing trade ("hope strategy"). If price touches your planned SL level, exit immediately without emotion.

⏳ 3. The 15-Day Time Stop Rule

Swing trades rely on short-term momentum (5 to 15 days). If a stock sits flat for 15+ sessions without hitting target or SL, exit the dead position and move capital to fresh breakouts.

🪂 4. Partial Profit & Trailing SL

When Target 1 (+5% to +8%) is reached, sell 50% shares to lock in gains. Instantly move your Stop Loss on remaining shares to your Buy Price (Breakeven) so you never turn a winning trade into a loss.

🇮🇳 Indian Stock Market Statutory Fee Reference

  • STT (Securities Transaction Tax): 0.1% on buy value AND 0.1% on sell value for equity delivery.
  • DP (Depository Participant) Fee: Fixed ₹15.93 per stock per sell day (CDSL/NSDL charge charged by brokers like Zerodha, Groww).
  • Exchange Transaction Charges: ~0.00345% of total turnover (NSE/BSE).
  • GST: 18% applied on Exchange charges and Brokerage.
